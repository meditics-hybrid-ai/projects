A Meditics, Inc. app

Request non-emergency care at home

Care On-Demand connects patients and families with nearby credentialed healthcare providers who can visit them at home, including nurses, doctors, caregivers, and physical therapists.

How it works

From request to home visit

  1. Tell us what care you needA patient or family member submits a home-care request.
  2. A nearby provider can acceptAvailable providers review the request and decide whether they can help.
  3. Follow the visit in the appAfter acceptance, check the visit status and track the provider’s arrival.

For healthcare providers

Offer home visits on your schedule

Submit your credentials for review, set your availability, and choose which nearby care requests you can accept.

Proposed Philippine outreach

Help residents enroll in person

Meditics is seeking permission from the Mayor’s Office to visit selected barangays. With permission, our team would work with barangay chairpersons to demonstrate the app and help residents enroll, including those without smartphones.
