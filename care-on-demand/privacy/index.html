Privacy Policy Draft

This is a product draft for review. It is not legal advice. Have counsel review before publishing or submitting the app.

Effective date: December 31, 2026

Overview

Care On-Demand is operated by Meditics, Inc. The app helps patients request home-health services and helps providers manage care requests, communication, and related payment records.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Account information such as name, email address, phone number, role, and address
  • Care request information such as requested service type, urgency, location, notes, status, and history
  • Provider profile and verification information such as provider type, credentials, review status, and uploaded documents
  • Communications between patients, providers, admins, and support workflows
  • Payment-related information such as payment method, amount, status, GCash reference, cash note, MDTX wallet transaction, and reconciliation status
  • Technical information needed for authentication, security, diagnostics, app operations, and advertising delivery when ads are enabled

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Match patients with appropriate providers
  • Display role-specific patient, provider, and admin workflows
  • Manage care request status, communication, and notifications
  • Record GCash, cash, and MDTX payment activity
  • Review providers, trust status, and operational issues
  • Protect the app, investigate errors, and improve reliability
  • Display ads only in non-clinical dashboard areas when advertising is enabled

Payments

The Philippines-first MVP records GCash payment references, cash notes, and MDTX wallet transactions. Insurance, credit card, and GoTyme payment methods are deferred from the first release.

If a payment gateway is later enabled, payment provider terms and privacy practices may also apply. Do not enter live payment credentials in test builds unless live payment processing is explicitly enabled.

Advertising

Care On-Demand may display advertising in limited non-clinical dashboard areas if advertising is enabled for a release. Ads should not be placed in urgent care, care-request creation, payment checkout, communications, visit documentation, or other sensitive workflows.

The current app is configured to request non-personalized ads and to use Google AdMob test identifiers by default. Before enabling live ads, production AdMob identifiers, App Store privacy disclosures, user-facing policy language, and an inappropriate-ad reporting path must be finalized.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate hosting, databases, payments, app review, diagnostics, support, advertising delivery if enabled, and security. We do not sell personal information.

Information may also be shared when required for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.

Retention

We retain account, care request, communication, provider verification, payment, and operational records as needed to provide the service, support users, reconcile payments, meet legal obligations, and protect the platform.

Retention windows and deletion procedures must be finalized before public launch.
