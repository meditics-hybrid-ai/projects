Privacy Policy for Meditics BINGO

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Meditics AI ("Meditics," "we," "us," or "our") provides the Meditics BINGO mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information You Provide

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, health information, or payment information.

Your bingo card, called numbers, marked spaces, and game progress are used only to run the current game. The current version does not send this gameplay information to Meditics servers or store it in a remote database.

Information Collected Automatically

The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect information from your device:

  • Google AdMob: The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive device and advertising identifiers, IP address, general location inferred from the IP address, information about ads shown or selected, app interactions, and diagnostic information. They may use this information to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and—where permitted and consented to—personalize advertising.
  • App update checks: The App requests a small configuration file hosted on GitHub to determine whether a newer version is available. As with a typical internet request, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address, request date and time, and user-agent or device-related request information.
  • Text-to-speech: The App uses device text-to-speech capabilities to announce game events and numbers. Handling by the device's speech service is subject to the privacy terms of your operating-system or speech-service provider.

Meditics does not use an analytics SDK in the current version of the App.

How Information Is Used

Information handled through the third-party services above may be used to:

  • operate and maintain the App;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • detect invalid traffic, abuse, and technical problems;
  • check whether an App update is available; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Sharing of Information

Meditics does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners, GitHub, and your device's speech-service provider for the purposes described above. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transfer.

For details about Google's practices, visit Google's Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. For details about GitHub's practices, visit the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising Choices and Consent

Depending on your location and device settings, you may be able to limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier through your Android or iOS privacy settings.

Where required, an advertising consent message should be presented before data is used for personalized advertising. If the App presents a privacy-options entry, you can use it to review or change your advertising choices. You can also contact us using the method below if you need help with a privacy choice.

Data Retention and Deletion

Meditics does not maintain user accounts or a remote database of gameplay information, so there is no App account or cloud gameplay record for us to delete. Current game information remains in the App's working memory only for the active session and is discarded when the App process ends or the game is reset.

Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. Requests concerning data controlled by a third-party provider should be directed to that provider. You may also contact us with a privacy or deletion question, and we will respond regarding any information under our control.

Data Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by the App. Internet transmission and electronic storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure, and third-party services maintain their own security practices.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Age thresholds and rights may differ by country.

International Processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy notices describe their international data-transfer practices.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because Meditics does not maintain App accounts or a gameplay database, we may not hold information that can be linked to you. To submit a privacy inquiry, use the contact method below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or legal requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date and make the revised policy available through the App and the public policy page. Material changes may also be communicated in the App or through the applicable app store.

Contact Us
